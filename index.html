Free Bulk Background Remover for Multiple Photos

  • Bulk in one click — process dozens of photos together, not one by one.
  • Blue background for admissions — the exact clean blue many schools & jobs require.
  • Passport sizes built in — Pakistan 35×45mm, US 2×2in, and custom.
  • Totally private — the AI runs in your browser; photos are never uploaded.
  • No watermark — clean, ready-to-use output every time.

How to Remove Backgrounds and Make Passport Photos

  1. Add one or many photos above.
  2. Pick the blue swatch (or your exact color).
  3. Choose your passport size (e.g. Pakistan 35×45mm).
  4. Click Remove backgrounds.
  5. Download each photo — or all of them as a ZIP.

About this project

Built by Umar Bhatti

Hi! I'm Umar Bhatti, a 6th-semester Computer Science student at COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus. I built BulkBGRemover to solve a real problem I kept seeing around me: students and job applicants needing clean passport photos with a proper background, but having to edit them one by one, or upload their private photos to a server.

This tool does it all right inside your own browser — remove backgrounds from many photos at once, set the exact background color, and crop to passport sizes. Your photos are never uploaded, so your data stays private. I built it to genuinely help people.

BulkBGRemover — background remover for passport & job photos.
